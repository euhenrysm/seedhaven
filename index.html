Seedhaven

Build, grow and trade.

Seedhaven is a farming game built around progression, resource management, crafting, exploration and player-driven ownership.

🏡
Build Develop and customize your farm.
🌾
Grow Plant, harvest and progress.
🤝
Trade Participate in a player-driven economy.

Our Vision

Seedhaven is designed to be a game first.

Players begin with a small piece of land and gradually transform it through farming, resource collection, upgrades, crafting and new discoveries.

Blockchain technology is intended to complement the game rather than define the entire experience.

Core principle: Players should be able to enjoy Seedhaven without needing previous knowledge of cryptocurrency, wallets or blockchain technology.

Gameplay Philosophy

The Seedhaven experience begins with an intuitive farming loop and gradually introduces deeper systems as the player progresses.

Core Gameplay Loop
Buy → Plant → Water → Grow → Harvest → Sell → Level Up → Unlock

Future progression may introduce resource gathering, crafting, buildings, animals, quests, events, collectibles, trading and player-owned assets.

The First Five Minutes

1. Buy your first seeds

The player begins with 100 Gold and purchases four Sunflower Seeds for 5 Gold each.

Balance: 100 Gold → 80 Gold

2. Plant

The four seeds are planted in the player's four available farming plots.

3. Water

The Watering Can is introduced and each crop is watered, beginning its growth cycle.

4. Explore while crops grow

Instead of forcing the player to wait, Seedhaven introduces a small secondary activity.

The player removes a fallen branch and receives 2 Wood and XP.

5. Harvest

After 30 seconds, the four Sunflowers are ready and can be harvested.

6. Sell

Each Sunflower can currently be sold for 10 Gold.

Four Sunflowers generate 40 Gold, taking the player's balance from 80 Gold to 120 Gold.

7. Level Up

Completing the first tutorial sequence takes the player to Level 2 and unlocks Potato Seeds.

Early Progression

Once the tutorial is complete, the player gains control of their farm and receives their first open objectives:

🌱 Plant 8 crops
🪙 Earn 250 Gold
⭐ Reach Level 3

Future progression will gradually introduce new crops, resources, crafting systems, buildings, expansions and additional gameplay mechanics.

Seedhaven Economy

Seedhaven uses a dual-currency economy.

The everyday gameplay economy and the blockchain economy are intentionally separated.

🪙

Gold

Seedhaven's main off-chain gameplay currency.

  • Earned through normal gameplay
  • Used to purchase seeds
  • Used for upgrades and crafting
  • Used for buildings and expansions
  • No fixed maximum supply
🌱

Seedhaven Coin — SHV

The planned on-chain ecosystem currency.

  • Built for the Seedhaven Web3 ecosystem
  • Planned for the Ronin Network
  • Potential NFT and marketplace utility
  • Controlled distribution
  • Tokenomics are not yet finalized
No automatic Gold → SHV conversion is currently planned. This separation helps prevent normal farming activity from creating unlimited quantities of blockchain tokens.

Ronin Network

Seedhaven plans to use the Ronin Network for selected blockchain functionality.

Most everyday gameplay data will remain off-chain so that Seedhaven can remain fast, accessible and easy to understand.

NFTs

NFTs are planned as an additional ownership layer rather than a requirement for every game action.

Potential NFT categories being explored include:

🏡 Special land assets
🐾 Rare animals or pets
👕 Cosmetic items
🗿 Collectibles
🏆 Event rewards
🏠 Special buildings or decorations

Common crops and basic resources are not currently planned to exist individually on-chain.

Development Roadmap

Phase 1

Core Farming

Farming plots, crops, planting, watering, growth, harvesting, selling, Gold, XP, levels and tutorial.

Phase 2

Farm Progression

More crops, Wood, Stone, resource gathering, crafting, buildings and farm expansion.

Phase 3

Living Farm

Animals, additional areas, quests, events, collections and social systems.

Phase 4

Ronin Integration

Wallet integration, blockchain inventory, selected NFTs and digital ownership systems.

Phase 5

Seedhaven Economy

SHV integration, token distribution, marketplace systems, player trading, ecosystem rewards and token utility.
