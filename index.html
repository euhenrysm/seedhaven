Our Vision

Seedhaven is designed to be a game first. Players begin with a small piece of land and gradually transform it through farming, resource collection, upgrades, crafting and new discoveries.

Core principle: players should be able to enjoy Seedhaven without needing previous knowledge of cryptocurrency, wallets or blockchain technology.

Gameplay Philosophy

The core loop is:

Buy → Plant → Water → Grow → Harvest → Sell → Level Up → Unlock

First 5 minutes

The player starts with 4 farming plots, 100 Gold, a Watering Can, a Farm Shop, Farm Stand and inventory.

During the tutorial, the player buys 4 Sunflower Seeds, plants, waters, collects 2 Wood while waiting, harvests the crops, sells them and reaches Level 2.

Seedhaven Economy

Seedhaven uses a dual-currency model so normal farming does not continuously create blockchain tokens.

🪙 Gold

Main off-chain gameplay currency.

  • Seeds
  • Buildings
  • Crafting
  • Upgrades
  • Farm expansion

🌱 Seedhaven Coin — SHV

Planned on-chain ecosystem currency.

  • Ronin Network
  • Selected NFT utility
  • Marketplace activity
  • Controlled distribution
  • Tokenomics TBD
No automatic Gold → SHV conversion is currently planned.

Ronin Network

Seedhaven plans to use Ronin for selected blockchain functionality. Common gameplay data such as crops, Gold, Wood, Stone and XP remains off-chain.

NFTs

Potential NFT categories include special land assets, rare animals or pets, cosmetics, collectibles, event rewards and special buildings or decorations.

Not every item needs to become an NFT.

Wallet integration, NFTs and ownership systems.
